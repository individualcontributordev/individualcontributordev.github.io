Final Fantasy VII — CSR

CutScenes Removed patches for the PS1 (and PC) versions of Final Fantasy VII. Same game, far less downtime — ideal for racing and casual replays alike.

  • CSR — skill checks stay; FMVs and long sequences are cut or shortened. Plays like a normal run, just faster.
  • CSR+ — removes cutscenes more aggressively, even when that changes familiar strats.
  • CSR++ — CSR+ plus filler dialogue and padding trimmed out.
