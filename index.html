CSR

CutScenes Removed for NTSC-U Final Fantasy VII (PS1). Apply packs with the disc builder.

  • CSR — skill checks kept; FMVs and long sequences cut or shortened. A base.
  • CSR+ scene add-ons — individual, mixable trims that go further than CSR for a specific scene. Apply on top of the CSR base.
  • Highwind — an aggressively trimmed playthrough: story mechanics, option choices, and complete dialogue cut. Its own separate base, not a bigger CSR+ — doesn't stack with CSR+ scene add-ons.
